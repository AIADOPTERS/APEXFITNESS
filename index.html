The race

What is HYROX?

A global fitness race: run 1km, complete one functional station, repeat eight times. Every event uses the same format, so results feed into international leaderboards and a World Championships each season.

  1. SkiErg
  2. Sled Push
  3. Sled Pull
  4. Burpee Broad Jumps
  5. Rowing
  6. Farmers Carry
  7. Sandbag Lunges
  8. Wall Balls

How we train for it

The HYROX pathway

Aerobic capacity plus the strength and skills for each station. Sessions are mostly whole-body, run in 12-week blocks, with a full race simulation at least once in every block.

HYROX class guideSuggested PB week

How long do I need?

2 to 3 months if your goal is to enjoy the day and finish.

6+ months of consistent training if you're chasing a PB.

How ELEVATE works

Compound first

Key compound lifts with accessory work, for a solid strength foundation.

Conditioning built in

Short and long bouts of capacity work to build fitness alongside strength.

Load, managed

Upper or lower body focus, with intensity and load managed across the week to avoid overtraining and reduce injury risk.

FAQs

Getting started

Is Apex beginner friendly for HYROX?

Absolutely. We recommend starting with HYROX Strength and Conditioning before trying the more challenging classes.

What's the best class for someone starting out?

If you're just starting your fitness journey, we recommend ELEVATE classes to build base strength, then going from there.

Is the gym only for HYROX athletes?

Not at all. Plenty of members don't race, they just enjoy the training. ELEVATE is our pathway for building strength and muscle.

HYROX

I'm well trained and want to PB. What do you recommend?

Build a consistent running base, then aim for 2 HYROX Strength and Conditioning, 1 Simulation, 1 Engine or Chipper, and 1 Compromised Running or Hybrid Method session a week.

How long do I need to train for a HYROX?

To enjoy the day and finish, 2 to 3 months may be enough. For a PB, plan on 6+ months of consistent training.

How are your HYROX classes different from bootcamp or cardio classes?

Every session is designed around the movements, pacing and structure of the race, and planned in phases by our PhD head coach.
